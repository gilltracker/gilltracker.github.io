Privacy Policy

Expense-email-parser. Last updated 25 October 2026.

This tool is used privately by a household. Only people personally invited by the operator use it. This page explains what it does with your Gmail data.

What it accesses

  • It asks Google for read-only access to your Gmail. It cannot send, delete, move or change any email.
  • It looks for card transaction alert emails from the banks you use, and reads those to find the amount, the merchant and the date of each purchase.
  • To find those alerts it reads the sender and similar details of incoming emails. It does not store the content of any other email.
  • Occasionally, to fix problems and only with your agreement, the operator may run a check that reads emails in a folder you name. Nothing from that check is stored except totals.

What it stores

  • For each purchase: amount, merchant name, date, a spending category, and a reference to the email it came from. Any corrections or notes you add are stored too.
  • Everything is kept on a private server owned by the operator, reachable only over a private network, not over the open internet.
  • Data is kept until you ask for it to be deleted.

What it never does

  • It does not sell, share or give your data to anyone.
  • It does not use your data for advertising.
  • It does not use your data to train artificial intelligence models.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • You can stop access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can ask the operator to delete everything stored about you, and it will be deleted.

Contact

Contact the person who invited you to use this tool.
